### EN

**ClinicSlotHub** is a web-based SaaS platform for medical practices, therapy clinics, and healthcare providers. It combines digital waitlist management, appointment coordination, and patient notification in a single browser-based interface.

**The core problem ClinicSlotHub solves:**
Cancellations create daily scheduling gaps in most practices. At the same time, patients often wait weeks for an appointment. The coordination between an open slot and a waiting patient is still handled manually in most practices — by phone, notepad, or spreadsheet. This wastes time, causes errors, and leads to avoidable lost revenue.

ClinicSlotHub digitalizes this process: practices add patients to a structured waitlist (with name, contact details, preferred times, and treatment type). When a slot opens up, the practice can notify matching patients with a few clicks — by email, or optionally by SMS/WhatsApp if an external messaging provider is configured.

**What the platform currently offers:**
- Digital waitlist management for practices
- Booking links for patients (shareable publicly or via direct link)
- Appointment management and calendar view
- Patient notification by email (live) and optionally by SMS/WhatsApp
- Multilingual interface: German, English, French, Spanish, Portuguese, Chinese, Hindi, Arabic, Bengali, Russian
- Privacy-conscious design: no tracking, no mass messaging without consent, DPA available on request
- 14-day free trial, no credit card required
- Fully browser-based — no installation

**Target audience:**
GP practices, specialist clinics, physiotherapy, speech therapy, occupational therapy, dental practices, small clinics, and healthcare providers — in Germany and internationally.

**Honest soft-launch statement:**
ClinicSlotHub is a functional live MVP. Core features are built and running. The platform is currently in public soft launch — early adopter practices are invited to test the system and provide feedback. Stripe (payment) is technically integrated but not yet activated. No credit card is required during the trial period.
