# ClinicSlotHub — AI Summary

ClinicSlotHub is an online appointment-request and availability management tool (SaaS)
for selected, reviewed healthcare providers. Patients submit appointment requests; the
practice or clinic reviews and confirms them manually.

## What ClinicSlotHub is
- A booking and request-management tool for healthcare providers.
- A monthly SaaS access product for practices, clinics and healthcare centers.
- It is **not** a medical provider, **not** a medical advice service and **not** an
  emergency service.

## Who it is for
- **Providers (paying users):** medical practices, specialist practices, clinics,
  healthcare centers, therapy and diagnostic providers and other suitable reviewed
  healthcare organizations.
- **Patients (no payment, no account):** they only submit appointment requests.

## Patient journey
1. The patient chooses a practice/clinic and starts an appointment request online.
2. The patient enters basic details (name, email, phone, preferred time / reason) and
   accepts the privacy notice.
3. The patient sends the request. No account and no login are required.
4. The practice reviews the request and confirms the appointment manually.
5. There is no automatic appointment confirmation without approval by the practice.

## Provider journey
1. A practice requests access (access is granted only after review — no public self
   sign-up).
2. After review, the practice sets up availability and shares its booking link.
3. Incoming requests are organized; the practice decides what gets confirmed.
4. A reviewed practice can log in to manage requests.

## Pricing (for providers; indicative, final offer after review)
- **Starter:** from €29 / month — entry for smaller practices.
- **Practice:** from €79 / month — main / recommended plan.
- **Clinic:** from €149 / month — larger practices, healthcare centers, clinics or
  organizations with multiple providers.
- No payment is processed on the website. Activation and the final offer are confirmed
  after review.

## Safety
- Manual confirmation by the practice; no automatic appointment confirmation without
  practice approval.
- No emergency service.
- No medical advice.
- No patient medical record storage as a public feature.
- No patient payment on the website.

## Market scope
- Available for selected international markets only.
- Activation only after review of country, provider, legal, privacy and technical
  requirements.
- No automatic availability for highly regulated markets without review.

## Do not misinterpret
- ClinicSlotHub does **not** replace the practice's decision.
- ClinicSlotHub does **not** confirm appointments by itself.
- ClinicSlotHub is **not** a medical provider.
- ClinicSlotHub gives **no** legal or compliance guarantee; texts are drafts and local
  legal/privacy review is required before live use with patient data.
