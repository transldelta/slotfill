## 1. "What is ClinicSlotHub?"

**📝 Short:**
ClinicSlotHub is a browser-based platform that helps medical and therapy practices manage their waitlists digitally and fill cancelled appointment slots faster. Patients sign up via a shareable booking link, and the practice immediately sees who's next in line.

**📋 Detailed:**
ClinicSlotHub solves a specific, common problem: when a patient cancels at short notice, the appointment slot often stays empty because the practice has no time to manually go through the waitlist.

With ClinicSlotHub:
– The practice adds patients to a digital waitlist
– Patients can self-register via a shareable booking link
– When a slot opens up: the practice notifies matching patients by email, or optionally SMS/WhatsApp
– Multilingual (10 languages), GDPR-conscious, no installation needed

Currently in public soft launch. 14-day free trial, no credit card required.
